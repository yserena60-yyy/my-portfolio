How to read

Start with all claims. Click a line to see whether two records are linked and what happened to each claim, with the words compared. Click a claim name to follow it through the whole genealogy.

Lines: is there a link?

link certain: documented that the later record drew on the earlier one, or details match that could only come from it
link likely: one strong trace, such as a shared error or the same unusual wording
link uncertain: a weak trace only

Cards: where the claim comes from

first recordedthe earliest record found with this claim
copythe claim was taken from an earlier record (faded card)
copy · error / firmer / softertaken from an earlier record, but miscopied, or made to sound more or less certain
agreesthe same claim as an earlier record, with no sign that it was taken from it (no link, or the record gives its own grounds)
new claim · sourceda different claim that names its basis (an examination, a publication)
new claim · unsourceda different claim with no basis named

With all claims shown, a card counts its claims by kind, e.g. “3 first” = three of its claims are first recorded here. On a line, “copied ×2” = two claims were copied at this step. The border takes the most notable kind (red, then purple, gold, blue, green, white).

Words on the lines

copied, copied?taken over unchanged (? = through a record not seen)
more certaina qualifier such as “possibly” was dropped
doubt softeneda strong doubt became a mild one
errora slip in copying
droppedthe claim is left out
addedthe claim appears for the first time

Where the evidence fits two outcomes, both are shown, e.g. “error / new claim · unsourced”.

Buttons

Links show or hide lines by how sure the link is. Judgements show or hide what is said about the claims (copy, error, new claim) by how sure that judgement is. “Well supported”: the wording settles it. “Inferred”: likely. “Tentative”: the evidence fits more than one reading.

Other marks

Dashed card with NOT SEEN: known only from a citation. PRIVATE: from a privately shared copy; wording withheld. Red tag “▲ more certain”: a claim became more certain at this step with no new grounds. Faded card: its judgements are hidden by the buttons, or it is outside the selected line of records. The record’s own caution (“possibly”, “?”, “c.”) is shown when you open a card.
