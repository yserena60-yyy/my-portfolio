Evidential Cartography of Eurasian Porcelain Networks

Uncertainty-Encoded Spatial Trade Mapping

Architected a spatial network visualization prototype tracing the multi-century circulation of East Asian export porcelain across early modern Eurasian trade corridors. Critically, the system encodes evidentiary uncertainty directly into cartographic variables — line opacity, stroke weight — rather than smoothing archival gaps into false continuity. Normalized legacy metadata from the British Museum and Rijksmuseum collections.

QGIS Mapbox Studio Wikidata SPARQL Mapbox GL JS Python
02
Cultural Heritage HCI Spatial Navigation Flutter · Supabase

CultureMap — Heritage Exploration System

Cross-Platform Gamified Spatial Learning

Engineered a cross-platform mobile and web application that transforms physical encounters with cultural landmarks into structured, data-rich educational experiences. Aggregates heritage metadata via Wikidata SPARQL and Wikipedia REST API. Architected a PostgreSQL-backed community annotation layer via Supabase. Deployed OSRM-powered multi-modal navigation — operationalizing informal public education through embodied spatial movement.

Flutter Mapbox GL Supabase Wikidata SPARQL OSRM SQLite

03
Spatial Humanities AI Vision HCI Three.js · WebGL

Mythic Arcanum — AI-Mediated Spatial Humanities

Irish Mythological Cycles · Computer Vision Interaction

Immersive, browser-deployed spatial humanities application mapping the four epochal cycles of Irish mythology across a real-time Three.js celestial environment. Synthesized touchless AI interaction via MediaPipe hand-tracking — enabling gesture-driven navigation directly applicable to accessible museum installations. IMAX-style cinematic video theater with GSAP camera animations delivers structured mythological narrative as a spatially sequenced educational arc.
